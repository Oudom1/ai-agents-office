DAY 1Tuesday · 29 December 2026

Changi Arrival, Jewel Waterfall & Clarke Quay

Arrive at 12:30 PM · Airport lunch and Jewel sightseeing before Heritage Collection on Seah (39 Seah Street)

  • 12:30 PMArrive at Singapore Changi Airport
  • 12:30–1:30 PMImmigration, baggage collection and arrival formalities (allow extra time if queues are long)
  • 1:30–2:30 PMLunch at Jewel Changi Airport, such as Food Republic on basement level 2; try chicken rice, laksa or another local dish
  • 2:30–3:00 PMTake photos at the Jewel Rain Vortex waterfall and enjoy a short Forest Valley walk
  • 3:00–4:15 PMTravel to Heritage Collection on Seah at 39 Seah Street by taxi/Grab or MRT (allow for bags and transfers)
  • 4:15–5:30 PMComplete mobile hotel check-in, unpack and rest
  • 5:30–6:15 PMOptional easy walk around the Bugis / City Hall neighbourhood, or rest longer if tired
  • 6:15–8:00 PMExplore Clarke Quay and the Singapore River; dinner at Clarke Quay or Boat Quay
  • 8:00–9:00 PMWaterfront photos and return to the hotel, or finish early after your flight

Heritage Collection on Seah lists check-in from 3 PM and uses mobile check-in; confirm access instructions and notify the property of your arrival time. Jewel is convenient for lunch and sightseeing before leaving the airport. Times are estimates and may shift with immigration and travel. Budget lunch roughly S$20–40 for two depending on your meal choices.

Check photo/security restrictions and holiday-period opening arrangements. Reserve cable car tickets. Dinner around S$50–75 for two.

DAY 4Friday · 1 January 2027

Scenic Bicycle Ride & Gardens by the Bay

Relaxed New Year's Day cycling around Marina Bay, followed by food, sightseeing and Garden Rhapsody

  • 9:30–10:30 AMLate breakfast and rest after New Year's Eve
  • 10:30–11:00 AMTravel to Marina Bay / Gardens by the Bay and collect rental bicycles (reserve ahead)
  • 11:00 AM–12:30 PM🚲 Easy scenic cycle on permitted paths: Gardens by the Bay → Marina Barrage → Gardens by the Bay East waterfront → return. Approx. 5–8 km depending on the route
  • 12:30–1:30 PMReturn bikes and enjoy lunch near Marina Bay
  • 1:30–3:30 PMVisit Chinatown for heritage streets and shops, or return to the hotel for a rest
  • 3:30–5:00 PMOptional Fort Canning Park visit or free time; Bugis shopping if you still have energy
  • 5:00–6:30 PMGardens by the Bay: Flower Dome or Cloud Forest (book tickets if needed)
  • 6:30–7:30 PMDinner around Marina Bay / Gardens by the Bay
  • 7:45–8:00 PMGarden Rhapsody light show (confirm holiday schedule)
  • 8:15 PMReturn to hotel

🚲 For 2 travelers: rent two bikes from an operating rental point; check January 1 opening hours, return location, rental terms and public-holiday availability in advance. Cycle only where permitted, use lights after dark, and follow local cycling rules. The route length is approximate; plan for heat, rain and crowds. If you're tired after the midnight countdown, move the ride later or shorten it.
